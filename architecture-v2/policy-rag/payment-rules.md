# Payment Terms and Rules

## Standard Payment Terms
RTL's standard payment term is Net 30 from invoice date. Net 60 applies to capital
trailer purchases over 50,000 CAD. Per-incident roadside and emergency reefer
dispatches are paid Net 15.

## Upfront Payment Cap
RTL caps upfront payments at 20 percent of total contract value. Exceptions require
CFO written approval. 100 percent upfront payment is never permitted.

## Harmonized Sales Tax (HST)
The HST rate in Ontario is 13 percent (5 percent federal GST plus 8 percent Ontario).
US vendors do not collect HST.

## Approval Thresholds and Authority Limits
Under 5,000 CAD: Shop Supervisor. 5,000 to 25,000: Fleet Manager. 25,000 to 100,000:
Controller. 100,000 to 500,000: CFO. Over 500,000: CEO with board notification.
Emergency roadside reefer repair: dispatch may authorize up to 10,000 CAD without
prior approval.
